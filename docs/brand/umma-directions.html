Umma Recommended

  • One word you can own. Nobody else in cooking is called this.
  • It means "mum" and sounds like "mama", so it reads even without Korean.

For comparison

"Moms", set the same way

moms

Same look, weaker name

  • Without the icon, "moms" says nothing about food. It could be a parenting forum or a daycare app.
  • "Moms" makes the user the mum. "Umma" makes the recipe hers, which is the product.
  • Commercially, a distinctive word is easier to market than a common one. People search for "Umma app"; nobody can find "Moms app".
  • If clarity is the worry, keep Umma and let the tagline do the explaining.

The agent picks from our cards

We design about 8 cards: pantry list, recipe options, step, swap, timer, answer, safety, “did you mean”. The agent chooses the card and fills it with checked data. Motion makes it feel alive.

  • Every screen is designed and tested, in light/dark and at large text sizes
  • Safety notices are always the fixed, solid style
  • The agent can't invent a brand-new kind of screen

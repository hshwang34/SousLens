How it works

Three steps, in the order you’d do them.

  1. Record it once

    A creator writes the recipe as ingredients and numbered steps, with notes and warnings where they matter. Or they upload their own cooking video and SousLens drafts the steps, with a clip for each one.

    The creator reviews and edits every draft. Nothing is published until they approve it.

  2. Match your pantry

    Tell SousLens what’s in your kitchen by typing, speaking, or taking a photo. It suggests items and you confirm each one. Every recipe then shows what you can make, what’s missing, and what it isn’t sure about.

    A suggestion from a photo never goes into your pantry until you tap to confirm it.

  3. Cook with coaching

    Go one step at a time and have each step read aloud. Ask “what does fold mean?” or “can I use lime instead of lemon?” and the coach answers from this recipe and this step.

    You approve every swap, and the original recipe stays as written. Only you move to the next step.

Clear about what’s AI and what isn’t

Every step shows where it came from, and safety rules always win over the model.

  • Creator instructions

    Written by the recipe’s creator.

  • AI-extracted, creator-verified

    Drafted from the creator’s video, then checked by the creator before publishing.

  • AI-extracted

    Drafted by AI. Never shown as the creator’s own words.

  • AI suggested adaptation

    A swap the coach proposed for your kitchen. You approve it first.

“I can’t tell from a description or photo whether food is cooked.”

Ask if the chicken is done and this is what you hear, along with what the recipe says to look for and how to check with a thermometer.

  • Safe cooking temperatures come from a USDA table, never from the model. Poultry is 165 °F.
  • Allergies come first. A “free-from” label never clears an allergy match; the app asks you to check the package.
  • Recipes that pickle, cure or ferment keep their salt, acid and sugar as written.
  • Nothing is saved to your pantry, swapped or published without your tap.

Your kitchen stays yours

Your voice is transcribed on your iPhone. Photos of your pantry are analysed once and never stored.

Stays on your iPhone

  • Your voice. Apple’s on-device speech recognition turns it into text, and you see the text before it’s sent.
  • Spoken steps and answers, read aloud by your phone.

Analysed, never stored

  • Pantry photos and photos you take while cooking.
  • They’re held in memory while Google Gemini looks at them, on a paid plan that doesn’t train on your data, then discarded.

Kept until you delete it

  • Your account, pantry, saved recipes and cooking sessions.
  • Recipes you publish, and creator videos and step clips.
  • Deleting your account in the app removes all of it.

The camera only runs when you start it, and nothing records in the background. Read the privacy policy.

For the cook who knows it by heart

You know what “until glossy” looks like. SousLens helps you pass it on.

Upload your own cooking video, up to 15 minutes, and SousLens drafts the ingredients and steps with a timestamped clip for each step. You review every line before anything goes public.

Your name stays on the recipe. When you edit a published recipe, it becomes a new version, so nobody’s cook changes halfway through.

Your original video is kept until you delete it, which you can do from the recipe at any time.

Only upload video you made or have the right to share.

Questions

Who can join the beta?

Anyone with an iPhone on iOS 17.2 or later, until the 100 tester places are taken. You install it through Apple’s free TestFlight app and sign in with Apple.

Does it cost anything?

No. The beta is free.

Can it tell me if my food is safe to eat?

No. SousLens never judges doneness or safety from a photo or a description. It tells you what the recipe says to look for and how to check with a thermometer.

What happens to the photos I take?

They’re analysed in memory to suggest ingredients or answer your question, then discarded. They aren’t saved on our servers or used to train AI.

Does it still work if the AI is unavailable?

Yes. You can still browse recipes, keep your pantry, see what you can make and step through a recipe. The app tells you when AI help is off.

Do I need Meta glasses?

No. Glasses support is in development and isn’t in the beta. SousLens works on iPhone alone.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile in the app and delete your account. Your pantry, recipes, sessions and videos are deleted with it.
